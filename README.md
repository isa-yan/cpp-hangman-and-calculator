My project is kind of 2 different concepts smushed together. I made both a calculator and a hangman style game in cpp. It's my first cpp project(s)
and I strugggled through a lot of it due to the kind of foreginness of cpp to me. I particularly struggled on understanding the vectors and custom
functions of cpp. However, I eventually got it through and I'm pretty happy with it :D I don't quite know how to explain it in detail, however. 
It's just a console game of hangman.

I made it with VSCode and C++ mainly.

Next time I do a similar project I'd try my best to write better variable names that didn't confuse me and go in actually having a better grip
of the language. I am rather glad though, I feel like I came out with a better understanding of programming logic and cpp itself.

Other people can help contribute by adding a bit to make the UI more aesthetically pleasing or being able to support bigger words.

It can be deployed through clang++ and in vscode. 

to run:
clang++ -g hangman.cpp -o
./hangman
